To find the properties of a wave from its mathematical equation, we compare the given formula to the standard form of a traveling wave equation.

## 1. Standard Wave Equation
The standard form for a wave traveling in the positive x-direction is:
$$y(x,t) = A \sin(kx - \omega t)$$

Where:
* **$A$** = Amplitude
* **$k$** = Wave number ($k = \frac{2\pi}{\lambda}$)
* **$\omega$** = Angular frequency ($\omega = 2\pi f$)

**Given Equation:**
$$y(x,t) = 0.05 \sin(2\pi x - 50\pi t)$$

---

## 2. Step-by-Step Solution

### a) Amplitude ($A$)
By direct comparison with the standard form:
**$A = 0.05 \text{ m}$**

### b) Wavelength ($\lambda$)
From the equation, the wave number **$k = 2\pi$**.
Using the formula $k = \frac{2\pi}{\lambda}$:
$$2\pi = \frac{2\pi}{\lambda}$$
**$\lambda = 1 \text{ m}$**

### c) Frequency ($f$)
From the equation, the angular frequency **$\omega = 50\pi$**.
Using the formula $\omega = 2\pi f$:
$$50\pi = 2\pi f$$
$$f = \frac{50\pi}{2\pi}$$
**$f = 25 \text{ Hz}$**

### d) Wave Speed ($v$)
The wave speed can be calculated using the relationship $v = f \cdot \lambda$ or $v = \frac{\omega}{k}$.
Using $v = f \cdot \lambda$:
$$v = 25 \text{ Hz} \times 1 \text{ m}$$
**$v = 25 \text{ m/s}$**

---
