## 1. Finding the Velocity Before Collision
As the $0.5 \, \text{kg}$ block ($m_1$) slides down the frictionless track, its gravitational potential energy is converted entirely into kinetic energy.



### Calculation:
Using the conservation of energy principle:
$$m_1gh = \frac{1}{2}m_1v_1^2$$

1.  **Solve for $v_1$:**
    $$v_1 = \sqrt{2gh}$$
2.  **Substitute values ($g = 9.81 \, \text{m/s}^2, h = 3.0 \, \text{m}$):**
    $$v_1 = \sqrt{2 \cdot 9.81 \cdot 3.0}$$
    $$v_1 = \sqrt{58.86} \approx 7.67 \, \text{m/s}$$

---

## 2. The Collision (Conservation of Momentum)
Since the blocks stick together, this is a **perfectly inelastic collision**. Total momentum is conserved.



### Variables:
* $m_1 = 0.5 \, \text{kg}$ (Moving block)
* $v_1 \approx 7.67 \, \text{m/s}$
* $m_2 = 1.5 \, \text{kg}$ (Stationary block)
* $v_2 = 0 \, \text{m/s}$
* $V_f = ?$ (Final velocity of combined mass)

### Calculation:
$$P_{initial} = P_{final}$$
$$m_1v_1 + m_2v_2 = (m_1 + m_2)V_f$$

1.  **Plug in the values:**
    $$(0.5 \cdot 7.67) + (1.5 \cdot 0) = (0.5 + 1.5)V_f$$
    $$3.835 = 2.0 \cdot V_f$$
2.  **Solve for $V_f$:**
    $$V_f = \frac{3.835}{2.0}$$
    $$V_f = 1.9175 \, \text{m/s}$$

**Answer:** The speed of the combined mass just after the collision is approximately **1.92 m/s**.
